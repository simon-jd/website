I build the systems under machine learning: GPU kernels, LLM inference and serving, and post-training. My recent projects include Crucible, an async GRPO loop that syncs its weights over NCCL across A10 nodes; kvsnap, KV-cache snapshots for vLLM; exactsample, batch-invariant sampling kernels in CUDA; and blockfp8, a block-scaled FP8 GEMM in CuTe. Outside the GPU, I work on network failover, Linux drivers and I/O runtimes, schedulers and data pipelines.

I'm studying for a Master of Science in Computer Science at Northeastern University in Seattle, and in 2026 I interned as a software engineer at SpectraCell Laboratories. Before the master's, I was a site reliability engineer at Yunwei Technology, on call for a queue-driven service on Kubernetes, and then a full-time research assistant at the Artificial Intelligence Institute of Wuhan University. My bachelor's degree, in computational and applied mathematics, is also from Wuhan University.

I graduate in May 2027 and am looking for new-grad roles in ML infrastructure and GPU systems, or in infrastructure and networking more broadly. I live in Seattle and am open to relocating anywhere in the US.
